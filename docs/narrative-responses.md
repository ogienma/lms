# Narrative responses: text, audio and video

How applicants give a written, audio or video response, and how it is reviewed. Written for
three groups: course creators (setting it up), learners (submitting) and reviewers.

**How it works, in one line:** the platform cannot take audio or video files. Learners type
their written response, and for audio or video they paste a **link** to a recording they
have put on another platform. Each response is set up as **two assignments**.

(Tracked in issue #33. The reasons and test results are there.)

## 1. For course creators: set it up

For each narrative response, create **two assignments** and put both in the lesson.

**Assignment A: the written response**
- In the sidebar open **Assignments** and create a new one.
- **Title:** for example "Case reflection: written response".
- **Submission Type:** **Text**.
- **Course:** choose the course.
- **Question:** the brief. Say clearly what to write, and add: *"Do not paste links to
  recordings into this box. Use the next assignment for that."*

**Assignment B: the recording link** (audio or video)
- Create another assignment. **Title:** for example "Case reflection: recording link".
- **Submission Type:** **URL**.
- **Question:** the brief, and the sharing instructions from section 2 (copy them in).
  Say that a recording is **optional or required**, whichever it is for this response.

Then add an **Assignment** block to the lesson for each one and choose the assignment.
Save, and open the lesson as a learner to check that both appear.

**Why two, and why Text and URL:** the Text type is the only way to collect typed answers,
and the URL type is the only one that keeps a recording link reliably. A link pasted into
the Text box can be lost when the answer is saved (see the limits below).

## 2. For learners: how to submit

### Your written response
1. Open the lesson and the assignment marked as the **written response**.
2. Type your answer in the box and press **Submit**.
3. **Do not paste links to recordings into this box.** Some links (YouTube, Google Drive,
   Vimeo) look fine while you type but are **deleted when you submit**, and you will not be
   told. Use the recording link assignment instead.
4. You can come back and change your answer while it still shows **Not Graded**.

### Your audio or video recording
1. Record your response with any tool you like.
2. Put it on a platform that gives you a **link**, for example YouTube (set to
   **Unlisted**), Loom, Google Drive (set to "Anyone with the link can view") or Dropbox
   (a shared link).
3. **Make it unlisted or link-only, never public.** Never upload client-related material
   to a public page.
4. **Test your link before you submit:** open it in a private (incognito) browser window. If
   you are asked to sign in or to request access, your reviewer will be asked too, and
   cannot see it. Fix the sharing setting first.
5. Open the **recording link** assignment, paste the link into the box (it must start with
   `https://`) and press **Submit**. You will see "Assignment submitted successfully".
6. If the link is wrong or needs changing, you can edit it while the submission still shows
   **Not Graded**.

**Keep your recording online** until you have been told your submission is graded. If you
delete it or make it private, your reviewer cannot see it.

**Remember:** "unlisted" is not private. Anyone who has the link can watch it, so do not
share it elsewhere.

## 3. For reviewers: how to review

1. Open the submission from **Assignments > Submissions**, or from the assignment.
2. **Written response:** the learner's text is shown in full on the page.
3. **Recording link:** the link appears in a **greyed-out box and is not clickable**. Select
   the link in the box, copy it, paste it into a new browser tab and open it.
   - If the box will not let you select the text, tell the course creator. This is a known
     inconvenience and has a planned fix, not yet done.
4. If the link asks you to sign in, request access, or has been removed, **do not grade**.
   Ask the learner to correct their sharing setting or link, and review once they have.
5. In the **Grading** section choose a grade (Pass or Fail), add **Comments**, and press
   **Save**.

## Limits you should know about

- **Reviewers cannot click the recording link.** They copy and paste it. We have not
  confirmed that copying works in every browser.
- **A link pasted into the written-response box can be lost.** YouTube, Google Drive and
  Vimeo links are turned into an embedded player and then deleted on save, silently. Loom
  and Dropbox links survived our test. Nothing stops a learner from doing it, so the
  instructions above tell them not to.
- **We keep no copy of the recording.** A link can break, be deleted or be made private
  later. If the platform removes it, the evidence is gone. If a recording must be kept for
  EMDRIA records, a reviewer needs to save a copy, which the platform does not do for you.
- **"Unlisted" is not private.** Anyone with the link can view it.
- **Not yet tried** with real learners, a real reviewer, other browsers, or private files.
- **Two small fixes would remove the first two problems:** show the submitted link as a
  clickable link for reviewers, and keep pasted video links as links in the text box. Neither
  has been done.
